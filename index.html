About Us

CONNECTING PEOPLE WITH URBAN SERVICES

Urban 360 is India's next-generation civic services coordination network, reducing the gap between public grievances and municipal action. We coordinate across roads, waste, water, sanitation, and electrical infrastructure.

Operating across 28 municipal areas, supporting over 48,000 resolved citizen requests through technology-driven routing and community transparency.
